## 🔌 Electronics & Embedded Work

### 📡 3D Scanner (Published)

An affordable **Arduino Uno** based 3D scanner that captures 3D coordinates and turns them into a model.

- **Hardware:** Sharp IR sensor, SD card module, motor control
- **Covers:** sensor calibration, scanning, data storage and point cloud processing
- **Applications:** manufacturing, prosthetics and digital twins
- **Published in:** *International Journal for Research in Applied Science & Engineering Technology (IJRASET)*, Volume 13, Issue V, May 2025
- **Links:** [Read the paper](https://www.ijraset.com/best-journal/3d-scanner) · [DOI: 10.22214/ijraset.2025.70429](https://doi.org/10.22214/ijraset.2025.70429)

### ☀️ Solar Rover

Solar-powered rover prototype controlled by Arduino, aimed at renewable energy and automation for remote sensing.

- **How it works:** DHT and humidity sensors monitor the environment, and servo motors handle movement
- **Tech:** Arduino UNO, NodeMCU, solar power, DHT sensor, servo motors

### 🔥 LPG Leak Guard

Safety system that detects LPG leaks and acts on them automatically to prevent accidents.

- **How it works:** an MQ6 sensor detects the leak and a buzzer sounds. The system sends SMS alerts through a GSM module, switches on an exhaust fan and shuts off the gas valve
- **Tech:** Arduino, MQ6 sensor, GSM module, buzzer, exhaust fan, valve shutoff

### 🔦 Laser Door Alarm

Security circuit that guards a door or an area against intrusion.

- **How it works:** a laser is aimed at an LDR, and when the beam is interrupted the alarm sounds instantly
- **Tech:** laser, LDR, alarm, beam detection
